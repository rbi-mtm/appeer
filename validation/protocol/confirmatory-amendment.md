# Confirmatory population-sampling amendment

This amendment was frozen before confirmatory frame retrieval or reference
harvesting.

The original development protocol allocated seven random and three
reference-enriched articles per journal-year. That allocation is unsuitable for
the confirmatory question because selecting articles with lifecycle references
would make external-metadata coverage artificially high.

The confirmatory corpus therefore draws ten articles per journal-year solely by
the deterministic DOI rank. The first four form the development split and the
remaining six form the locked holdout, preserving the planned totals of 500 and
750. Reference availability, appeer success, OA status, and article-history
completeness play no role in selection.

All 1,250 sampled records remain in the frozen corpus. Eligibility is classified
after freezing, while blinded to appeer dates and reference availability, using
`eligibility.md`. Ineligible records are reported as exclusions and retained for
false-inclusion and missingness analyses; they are not replaced.
